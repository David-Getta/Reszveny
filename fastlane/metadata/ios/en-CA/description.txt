Photograph a stock and learn everything about it.

A company logo on a shop front, a screenshot of your brokerage app, a newspaper's stock table or an old share certificate: WhatStock recognises the stock and shows its price, chart and key figures in seconds. Then an independent AI analysis explains it the way a good financial journalist would, in plain language and in your language.

WHAT THE ANALYSIS COVERS
• What the company does and what really moves the stock
• Valuation compared with its own history and its peers
• Risks, recent news and the next results date
• Three scenarios – bull, base and bear – with how likely each one looks
• The strongest counter-argument, so you see both sides
• Sources for the facts it uses
You choose the depth (brief, standard or deep) and the level (beginner or experienced). Afterwards you can ask follow-up questions about the analysis.

ON YOUR COMPUTER
On Mac and Windows, WhatStock lives in the menu bar or system tray: press the shortcut from anywhere, type a ticker or paste a screenshot, and the analysis opens.

120 LANGUAGES
The app and the analyses are available in 120 languages.

TRY IT
The first analyses are free. After that, a subscription or an analysis pack keeps it going.

WhatStock gives information, not investment advice. It does not know your situation, goals or tax position, so it describes trade-offs and scenarios rather than telling you what to buy or sell. Market data may be delayed.
