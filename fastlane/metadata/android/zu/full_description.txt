Thatha isithombe sesheya bese ufunda konke ngalo.

Ilogo yenkampani esitolo, isithombe-skrini sohlelo lwakho lobhuloka, ithebula lamasheya ephephandabeni noma isitifiketi esidala samasheya: i-WhatStock ilibona isheya futhi ngemizuzwana nje ikhombisa intengo yalo, ishadi nezibalo ezibalulekile. Bese uhlaziyo oluzimele lwe-AI lulichaza njengoba intatheli enhle yezezimali ingenza – ngolimi olulula nangolimi lwakho.

OKUQUKETHWE UHLAZIYO
• Lokho okwenziwa yinkampani nokuthi yini ngempela enyakazisa isheya
• Ukubaluleka kwalo uma kuqhathaniswa nomlando walo nezimbangi zalo
• Izingozi, izindaba zakamuva nosuku lwemiphumela elandelayo
• Izimo ezintathu – isimo sokukhuphuka, esiyisisekelo nesokwehla – nokuthi ngasinye singenzeka kangakanani
• Impikiswano eqine kakhulu, ukuze ubone zombili izinhlangothi
• Imithombo yamaqiniso asetshenzisiwe
Ukhetha ukujula (okufushane, okujwayelekile noma okujulile) nezinga (oqalayo noma onolwazi). Ngemuva kwalokho ungabuza imibuzo eyengeziwe ngohlaziyo.

LANDELELA KONKE
• Izintandokazi nosesho lwakamuva, nohlaziyo olugcinwe kudivayisi yakho
• Okushintshile kusukela ohlaziyweni lokugcina, ngokubuka nje
• Qhathanisa amasheya afika kwamathathu eceleni komunye nomunye
• Iphothifoliyo: ukuthenga, inzuzo, izabelo, inani ngokuhamba kwesikhathi, ukuguqulwa kwemali kanye nokungenisa nokukhipha i-CSV
• Ukubuyekezwa kwephothifoliyo nge-AI: ukugxila, ukuncika emikhakheni nasezimalini, izingozi ezihlanganyelwe
• Izexwayiso zentengo – nange-imeyili uma uhlelo luvaliwe
• Izinsuku ezizayo zemiphumela yamasheya akho, nesifinyezo samasonto onke nge-imeyili
• Amanothi akho ngesheya ngalinye
• Ngena nge-imeyili yakho ukuze konke kuvumelaniswe kuwo wonke amadivayisi akho

ZAMA
