Thatha isithombe sesheya bese ufunda konke ngalo.

Ilogo yenkampani esitolo, isithombe-skrini sohlelo lwakho lobhuloka, ithebula lamasheya ephephandabeni noma isitifiketi esidala samasheya: i-WhatStock ilibona isheya futhi ngemizuzwana nje ikhombisa intengo yalo, ishadi nezibalo ezibalulekile. Bese uhlaziyo oluzimele lwe-AI lulichaza njengoba intatheli enhle yezezimali ingenza – ngolimi olulula nangolimi lwakho.

OKUQUKETHWE UHLAZIYO
• Lokho okwenziwa yinkampani nokuthi yini ngempela enyakazisa isheya
• Ukubaluleka kwalo uma kuqhathaniswa nomlando walo nezimbangi zalo
• Izingozi, izindaba zakamuva nosuku lwemiphumela elandelayo
• Izimo ezintathu – isimo sokukhuphuka, esiyisisekelo nesokwehla – nokuthi ngasinye singenzeka kangakanani
• Impikiswano eqine kakhulu, ukuze ubone zombili izinhlangothi
• Imithombo yamaqiniso asetshenzisiwe
Ukhetha ukujula (okufushane, okujwayelekile noma okujulile) nezinga (oqalayo noma onolwazi). Ngemuva kwalokho ungabuza imibuzo eyengeziwe ngohlaziyo.

LANDELELA KONKE
• Izintandokazi ohlwini lwakho, usesho lwakamuva, nohlaziyo lwakho olugcinwe kudivayisi yakho
• Isifinyezo se-AI sansuku zonke sokuthi amasheya akho ahambe kanjani nokuthi kungani
• Okushintshile kusukela ohlaziyweni lokugcina, ngokubuka nje
• Qhathanisa amasheya afika kwamathathu eceleni komunye nomunye
• Umlando wemiphumela, ukuhweba kwabangaphakathi nokuthambekela kwabahlaziyi ekhasini lesheya ngalinye
• Iphothifoliyo: ukuthenga, inzuzo, izabelo, inani ngokuhamba kwesikhathi nokuguqulwa kwemali – ngenisa kusuka ku-CSV noma nje esithombeni-skrini somthengisi wakho wamasheya
• Inzuzo yakho yonyaka (elinganiswe ngemali), umgomo wephothifoliyo, ne-S&P 500 kunoma yiliphi ishadi ukuze uqhathanise
• Thumela ngaphandle inzuzo osuyitholile ukuze ugcwalise imbuyiselo yentela, futhi wengeze izinsuku zemiphumela ezizayo ekhalendeni lakho
• Ukwabiwa okuhlosiwe, nokuthi yini okufanele uyithenge noma uyithengise ukuze ubuyele kokuhlosiwe
• Ukubuyekezwa kwephothifoliyo nge-AI: ukugxila, ukuncika emikhakheni nasezimalini, izingozi ezihlanganyelwe
• Izexwayiso zentengo, nama-trailing stop – nange-imeyili uma uhlelo luvaliwe
• Izinsuku zemiphumela nezikhumbuzo zamasheya akho, nesifinyezo samasonto onke nge-imeyili
• Amanothi akho ngesheya ngalinye, nefayela lesipele ongaligcina
• Ngena nge-imeyili yakho ukuze konke kuvumelaniswe kuwo wonke amadivayisi akho
• Thayipha incazelo efana nokuthi “tech 100 ETF” bese i-AI ithola ukuthi usho liphi isheya noma isikhwama; ama-ETF nezikhwama zithola incazelo ngolimi olulula ethi “Yini lokhu?”
• “Kwakukhona ini embikweni wakamuva?” – isifinyezo se-AI semiphumela yekota nemithombo, nemiphumela emisha yamasheya akho ngokubheka kanye
• Buza imibuzo eyengeziwe nganoma iyiphi inothi ye-AI, nangokuqhathanisa
• Izabelo ezitholiwe: amanani aphelele alo nyaka nawaso sonke isikhathi, afakwe ngesandla noma avela ekukhishweni kwe-broker yakho, nge-CSV yembuyiselo yakho yentela
• Ngenkathi ungekho: ngemva kosuku noma ngaphezulu, ukunyakaza okukhulu kakhulu, izexwayiso ezivuliwe nemiphumela ememezelwe kusukela ekuvakasheni kwakho kokugcina
